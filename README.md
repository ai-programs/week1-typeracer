# 🚀 Week 1 — Build Your First Web App

Welcome to your first project! Today you're going to build something cool — and you're going to do it by talking to AI.

You won't be writing code yourself. Instead, you'll tell Cline (your AI assistant) what to build, test it, and ask for improvements. This is called **vibe coding** — and it's how a lot of real software gets built today.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Step 1: Open the Cline chat
Look for the **Cline icon** (a speech bubble with sparkle) on the left sidebar. Click it to open the chat.

### Step 2: Give Cline your first prompt
Based on the requirements given, describe what you want to build in your own words. The more specific you are, the better the result!

### Step 3: Check the preview
Look at the **Ports** tab at the bottom of the screen — your app should appear there! If not, wait a few seconds for it to load.

### Step 4: Test it!
Try your app. Does it work? Is there anything you'd change?

---

## 🔄 Make It Yours

Now comes the fun part — **iterate!** Ask Cline to change things. Make it look better. Add features. Experiment!

**Pro tip:** The more specific your prompt, the better the result. Instead of "make it look better", try "change the font to something modern, make the buttons rounded with a gradient, and add a subtle shadow".

---

## 🧠 What You're Learning

Without even realizing it, you're working with:

| Technology | What it does | Analogy |
|---|---|---|
| **HTML** | Structure of the page | The skeleton 🦴 |
| **CSS** | How it looks | The clothes 👕 |
| **JavaScript** | How it behaves | The brain 🧠 |

Every time you ask Cline to "add a button" — that's HTML.
Every time you say "make it blue" — that's CSS.
Every time you say "do something when I click" — that's JavaScript.

---

## 📁 Your Files

| File | What's inside |
|---|---|
| `index.html` | The structure of your app |
| `style.css` | The visual design |
| `main.js` | The app logic |

---

## 💡 Tips

- **Don't be afraid to break things.** You can always ask Cline to fix it.
- **Be specific.** "Add a timer" works. "Add a countdown timer that shows seconds and turns red in the last 5 seconds" works way better.
- **Experiment!** Try weird things. Ask for animations, sounds, themes. See what happens.
- **If something doesn't work**, tell Cline exactly what went wrong: "When I click the button, nothing happens" is much better than "it's broken".

Have fun! 🎮
